\# 06 — Interview Plan and questionaire



\*\*Project:\*\* Banking Loan Process — As-Is / To-Be  

\*\*Date:\*\* 25 September 2026







\## Standard pain questions (used in every interview)



\- \*\*P1.\*\* Tell me about the last time an application went wrong at your stage. What happened, and how often does something like that happen?

\- \*\*P2.\*\* When you are waiting on someone else (the customer, another team or a system), what do you do in the meantime?

\- \*\*P3.\*\* If you could fix one thing about this process tomorrow, what would you fix first, and why?



\## Standard wrap-up questions (used in every guide)



\- \*\*W1.\*\* What have I not asked about that I should have?

\- \*\*W2.\*\* Who else should I speak to, and are there any documents or reports that would help me understand this better?

\## 1 — Branch Sales Officer / Relationship Manager



\### Warm-up



1\. How long have you been in this role, and what does a typical day look like?

2\. Roughly what share of your time goes to personal loans compared with other products?

3\. How do customers usually first come to you about a personal loan?



\### Process



4\. Walk me through what happens from the moment a customer says they want a personal loan until the file leaves your hands.

5\. How do you decide which documents to ask a customer for?

6\. How do you capture the application in the system, and which parts are typed in manually?

7\. How do you check that a file is complete before sending it on?

8\. How does the file get to Credit Operations, and how do you know they have received it?

9\. When Credit Operations needs something more from the customer, how does that request reach you, and what do you do with it?

10\. How do customers find out the status of their application?

11\. What happens at your end after a loan is approved?

12\. (Confirm) So the customer never uploads documents directly — everything comes through you. Is that right?

\## Guide 3 — Underwriter



\### Warm-up



1\. How long have you been underwriting personal loans?

2\. What types of applications do you find straightforward, and which take the most thought?

3\. How does a file reach you, and in what condition does it usually arrive?



\### Process



4\. Walk me through how you assess a personal loan application from start to decision.

5\. How do you assess income and existing obligations?

6\. How do you calculate the debt-to-income ratio, and where do the inputs come from?

7\. Which parts of the credit policy do you apply most often, and how do you check them?

8\. What happens when an application falls outside policy?

9\. Who needs to approve a deviation, and how is that approval obtained?

10\. How do you record your decision and the reasons for it?

11\. What information do you find yourself re-checking that was already checked earlier?

12\. (Confirm) Every application, however simple, is reviewed manually by an underwriter. Is that right?

9\. How are funds released, and which system is used?

\## Guide 5 — Risk \& Compliance Officer



\### Warm-up



1\. How long have you worked in compliance, and which areas do you cover?

2\. At which points in the personal loan process are you involved?

3\. Which regulations shape this process most?



\### Process



4\. Walk me through the compliance checks that happen on a personal loan application.

5\. Which of these checks are required by regulation, and which are internal policy?

6\. How are AML and negative-list screenings carried out, and when in the process?

7\. How are fraud alerts raised and handled?

8\. What evidence must be kept in the file to show each control was performed?

9\. How do you check that the borrower received the required disclosures (such as the Key Fact Statement)?

10\. How is the final compliance check before disbursal carried out?

11\. Which controls could, in your view, happen earlier in the process without weakening them?

12\. (Confirm) The final compliance check happens after the credit decision, not before. Is that correct?
