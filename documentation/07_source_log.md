\## What I used from each source



\*\*S01 – RBI KYC Directions (2025)\*\*

\- KYC is mandatory before giving a loan: Aadhaar or another valid ID, plus PAN (or Form 60).

\- KYC can be done digitally, by video call, or by fetching the customer's record from the central KYC registry (CKYC).

\- Used for: A9 Verify KYC, A10 Validate PAN.



\*\*S02 – RBI Digital Lending Directions (2025)\*\*

\- The borrower must get a Key Fact Statement (KFS) before signing.

\- Signed documents must be sent to the borrower.

\- Money must go directly into the borrower's own bank account.

\- The customer's consent is needed before collecting their data.

\- Used for: A5 consents, A20 KFS, A22 agreement, A25 disbursal. These are controls the To-Be must keep.



\*\*S03 – ICICI: documents required\*\*

\- Salaried applicants need ID/address proof, PAN, 3 months' bank statement, 1 salary slip and a photo.

\- Approval "within 48 hours" \*\*only if documents are complete\*\*.

\- Used for: A3–A4 document collection, A7 completeness check.



\*\*S04 – SBI: Real-Time Xpress Credit\*\*

\- Fully digital loan with Aadhaar OTP e-sign for existing salary-account customers.

\- Loan range ₹1 lakh – ₹50 lakh; EMI can be at most 65% of monthly income.

\- Used for: A16 debt-to-income limit, and as proof that a digital To-Be is realistic.



\*\*S05 – Kotak: personal loan\*\*

\- "Approval takes 24–72 hours."

\- Asks for 3 salary slips (ICICI asks for 1), so document checklists differ between banks.

\- Used for: turnaround benchmark; one reason documents go missing.



\*\*S06 – ICICI blog: how long a loan takes\*\*

\- Approval: 2–3 working days. Disbursal: 1–2 working days after approval.

\- Lists the stages: apply → verify documents → verify identity → credit check → sanction → disburse.

\- Used for: the order of stages in the Level 1 and Level 2 models.



\*\*S07 – Omozing job description (Credit Operations)\*\*

\- Tasks: check documents are complete, assess eligibility, manage disbursal, keep records.

\- Used for: the Credit Operations lane; A7, A25, A26.

\- Limitation: an NBFC, not a bank.



\*\*S08 – HDFC job description (Credit Officer)\*\*

\- Credit staff are separate from sales and report to a Credit Manager.

\- They cross-check the application form against documents and flag possible fraud.

\- Used for: A11 cross-check, A13 fraud referral, A18 Credit Manager.

\- Limitation: rural group lending, not personal loans.



\*\*S09 – Newgen case study\*\*

\- After moving to a digital loan system, the bank's turnaround dropped to 48 hours.

\- Used for: checking that my \~2-day To-Be estimate is realistic.

\- Limitation: vendor marketing, about business loans, no "before" figure.
