**BUSINESS CONTEXT**(Hypothetical scenario)

Meridian Bank is a mid-sized retail bank offering unsecured personal loans of
range ₹1 lakh to ₹50 lakh to salaried and self-employed customers. Digital-first lenders now
approve comparable loans in minutes; Meridian advertises a 5-day turnaround
and frequently misses it. Branch staff spend a large share of their week
chasing documents rather than selling.

**BUSINESS PROBLEM**

Personal loan applications take 11.5 days end to end against a market
expectation of 2–3 days. A significant share of applications are abandoned by the
customer before decisioning, and a significant share of the work performed is
rework caused by incomplete or mismatched documents. The bank cannot say
which stage of its own process causes the delay.

**WHY IT MATTERS**

Slow origination loses winnable applications to faster competitors, inflates
cost per loan booked, and pushes operations cost up without adding any value
the customer can see.

**Note** on sources: Meridian Bank is fictional. This context and problem statement are based on publicly available documents saved in the evidence/ folder (RBI regulations, bank product pages, job descriptions and a vendor case study; see 07\_source\_log.md). Banks do not publish their internal loan processes, so the As-Is process in this project is a reconstruction from these sources, not a real bank's actual process. Figures that could not be sourced are estimates, recorded in analysis/assumption\_register.xlsx.
